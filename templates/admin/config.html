Welcome

When someone's first message is a single word, like a keyword she was asked to comment, the AI is not called at all. The welcome below is sent exactly as typed, and then it waits for her answer.

Any word works, so a new reel needs nothing set up here. The word tells us nothing about her, so it does not qualify her for anything: "READY" means she watched a reel to the end, not that she is ready to book. A longer first message, or a single word later in a conversation, goes to the AI.

Safety & Handover

Hard boundaries are enforced in code, not here: confirmed menopause, no uterus and both tubes unavailable without IVF remove the booking link from the prompt entirely, so it cannot be sent. A fertility lead aged 48 or over is handed to the team for review before the link, never rejected. Medication and surgery questions, requests for a human, and existing or former clients pause the AI automatically.

When a conversation is handed over, the AI writes nothing. It stops, pauses itself and tags the contact, and sends one of the lines below exactly as typed here. Abuse and spam get no message.
