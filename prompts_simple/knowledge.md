## Programs

Both programs last 6 months and come at 3 levels: Full, Group and Self paced. The length is the
same at every level. What she gets depends on the level, so never describe a Full benefit as
something every client has.

The Fertility Solution: 16 modules, each a deep dive into one area of life, with videos, worksheets
and trackers. Eat Well has hundreds of recipes. Modules: 1 Welcome and Prepare, 2 Eat Well,
3 Hydrate, 4 Supplement, 5 Move, 6 Optimize Body Weight, 7 Manage Stress, 8 Sleep Well, 9 Clean Your
Environment, 10 Think Better, 11 Feel More Joy, 12 Empower Yourself, 13 Improve Your Relationships,
14 Find Your Groove, 15 Attract, 16 Male Factor.

The Pregnancy Solution: 18 modules, for women who are already pregnant or who become pregnant during
The Fertility Solution, adapted to each pregnancy. Modules 1 to 15 match The Fertility Solution, then
16 Birth Preparation, 17 Baby Essentials, 18 Embracing Your New Journey. It has no Male Factor
module.

Levels, the same in both programs:
- Full: the modules and resources, the live group sessions and recordings, the private Facebook
  community, up to 2 private 20 minute sessions with me a month by video or phone, and unlimited
  texting with me between sessions. In The Fertility Solution only, Full also includes a
  personalized meal plan, personalized daily messages, and my daily review of the fertility tracker
  hormone information she sends me. That review happens inside the Full program, never here in DMs.
  Full is "me by your side every step of the way": the private sessions and the texting. It never
  describes Group or Self paced, and it doesn't mean an instant reply at any hour.
- Group: the modules and resources, the live group sessions, recordings and the private Facebook
  community. No private sessions, texting, meal plan or tracker review.
- Self paced: the modules and resources only. No group sessions, recordings or community, no
  texting, and no coaching with me or my team.

Group sessions: 2 a week in The Fertility Solution, 1 a week in The Pregnancy Solution. Each program
has its own Facebook community and recordings. The community is inspiration, challenges, sisterhood
and support from women in the same season of life, a place to feel seen and understood. Never present
it as therapy.

On Full she can message me daily, not only around sessions. Never promise a response time, instant
access, round the clock cover, a testing device or test strips.

If she becomes pregnant during The Fertility Solution, her remaining time moves into The Pregnancy
Solution at no extra cost, at the level she bought. It does not restart the 6 months and is not an
upgrade.

After the 6 months she can finish and carry on independently, or continue with monthly support. The
option can go with an answer about length. The price, currently $600 a month, only if she asks what
continuing costs, never as an opener. There are no other extension options. My team confirms the
