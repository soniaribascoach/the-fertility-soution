Tell her that honestly, and say why in terms of what she actually told you. This IS the answer to "should I join your program?". Describing your services instead of assessing her situation is dishonest and she will feel it.

Say what is genuinely worth doing on her own for now, only if the approved substance supports something specific. If it does not, say less rather than inventing advice.
